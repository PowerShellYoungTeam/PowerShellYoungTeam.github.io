I love PowerShell because it lets me make life easier by scripting as much as I can. I'm interested in Windows administration, automation, and visual tools that make useful information easier to scan.

> You can take the sysadmin out of the scheme, but you can't take the scheme out the sysadmin.

This site is a mix of practical admin scripts, PowerShell Gallery modules, dashboards, notes, and occasional experiments. I share what I'm learning through code and writing.

Some projects are forks, early experiments, or older scripts; their repository pages are the best place to check their current state.
